# NOVA Digital — International Prospecting Playbook

## Two acquisition lanes

### Lane A: Direct business clients
Target owner-led businesses whose websites are missing, outdated, difficult to use on mobile, or do not make enquiries/bookings easy.

Initial niches:
- Cleaning and home-service businesses
- Restaurants and catering businesses
- Consultants and professional services
- Small retail and product brands

### Lane B: Agency partnerships
Target small web design, branding, SEO and digital marketing agencies that may need extra implementation capacity.

Offer:
- Front-end implementation from approved designs
- Responsive website builds
- Website fixes and maintenance
- Product/catalogue and enquiry flows
- Dashboard and database-backed features where scoped

Be explicit about whether work is white-label, who owns client communication, confidentiality, source-code ownership and payment.

## Daily activity target
Start with a sustainable daily target:
- 5 carefully researched prospects
- 3 personalised first-contact messages
- 2 follow-ups to earlier prospects
- 1 portfolio or case-study improvement

Prioritise quality and relevance over mass messaging. Do not scrape personal data or send bulk unsolicited messages.

## Prospect qualification checklist
Before contacting a prospect, confirm:
1. The business or agency is real and active.
2. The person is relevant to the decision or referral.
3. There is a specific reason NOVA's service may help.
4. The prospect's website or public profile supports your observation.
5. Your message is personalised and respectful.

## Suggested lead stages
Researching → Ready to contact → Contacted → Replied → Discovery call → Proposal sent → Won / Lost / Follow up later.

## First 30-day experiment
- Build a list of 40 agencies and 40 direct businesses.
- Send a small number of personalised messages each working day.
- Record every contact and follow-up in `lead-tracker.csv`.
- Review replies, calls, proposals and wins weekly.
- Change the niche or message based on evidence, not assumptions.

Targets are operating goals, not promised conversion rates.
